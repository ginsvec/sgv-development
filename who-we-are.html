Our commitment

Our word is our foundation.

We only commit to what we can deliver, and we put it in writing.

  • Scope in writing

    Inclusions and exclusions are based on the drawings and the site visit, and agreed before we mobilize.

  • Dates we keep

    The sequence and the dates are set with you before we start. If a date is at risk, you hear it from us first.

  • A price that holds

    Lump sum or unit rates. Changes are priced and approved in writing before the work goes ahead.

Our values

What we stand for.

Four rules our crews work by on every job.

  1. 01

    Safety first

    Every task is assessed before it starts, and anyone on our crews can stop work that is unsafe.

  2. 02

    Workmanship

    Every pour, every wall and every finish is built to the drawings and the specifications, and checked before handover.

  3. 03

    Respect for the site

    We keep the site clean, protect finished work and respect the neighbours.

  4. 04

    Durability

    We build to last, not just to pass inspection.
