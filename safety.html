Construction safety · British Columbia Safety is our core value.

It is planned into every job from the start, not added afterwards. Everyone goes home safe.

Our standard

No schedule or price is worth an injury.

We identify the hazards of every task and put the controls in place before work starts. PPE is the last line of defence, not the first.

We plan and supervise our own work, so the safety of everyone doing it is our responsibility.

On site

How we work safely.

  • Safety program and orientation

    A written safety program and a site orientation before anyone starts work.

  • Training

    WHMIS, fall protection and equipment training, with records kept on file.

  • Daily toolbox talks

    Before every shift, our foreman reviews the day's tasks and hazards with the crew.

  • First aid

    Attendants and kits on site, as WorkSafeBC requires.

  • Stop-work authority

    Anyone on our crews can stop a task they believe is unsafe, without fear of reprisal.

  • Incident reporting

    Every incident and near miss is reported, investigated and reviewed with the crew.
